Device: ONEPLUS ACE 6T
Recommended Recovery: https://downloads.sourceforge.net/project/rab1d-s-roms/PROJECT_INFINITY_X/recoveries/infinity_macan_recovery.img
fastboot flash recovery infinity_macan_recovery.img

Flashing Instructions:

* Clean Flash:
1. Reboot to recovery [AOSP Recovery Recommended] (Get from here: https://sourceforge.net/projects/rab1d-s-roms/files/PROJECT_INFINITY_X/recoveries/infinity_macan_recovery.img)
2. Apply update -> adb sideload
3. adb sideload rom.zip
4. Reboot recovery
5. Format data
6. Reboot

* Dirty Flash:
1. Reboot to recovery
2. Apply update -> adb sideload
3. adb sideload rom.zip
4. Reboot

* OTA:
1. Simply upgrade via OTA updater (if pushed or by importing local update)
