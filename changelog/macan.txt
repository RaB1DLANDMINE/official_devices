Infinity-X — Changelog (1.08.2026)

Core & Base
- Infinity-X core 3.11 → 3.12 — latest platform, security & framework updates.
- OnePlus stock blobs 8.301 → 9.400 — newer firmware, HALs & camera stack (OxygenOS parity).
- SuperVOOC wired in for full-speed, OxygenOS-parity fast charging.

OOS Camera
- Full OxygenOS camera experience — 100% stable. Every known bug fixed: capture-time library failures, HEIF handling, and the depth-video/aperture-change audio glitch. No outstanding camera bugs.
- On-device Face Tracking & QR scanning — runs in-app, no AICore / OnePlus AI dependency.
- Thumbnail opens a gallery via Android's app picker (set a default with "Always").

Performance
- ZRAM on by default (¾ of RAM) for smoother multitasking.
- High Touch Sampling Rate for crisper, lower-latency input in games and scrolling.

Battery & Idle
- Aggressive deep-Doze — drops into deep sleep within minutes of screen-off and stays there; much less overnight drain, with background apps auto-restricted instead of waking the phone all night.

Display & Ambient
- Smarter auto-brightness — no more pocket-to-daylight lag or too-dim indoors; recovers faster and brighter.
- AOD Raise-to-Wake toggle (InfinitySuite).

Audio
- Dolby Atmos — now fully working on 15R/A6T for the first time despite no official OEM support. Dolby actually processes the audio, with live EQ, sound profiles, virtualizers and advanced settings, plus per-device memory. Includes AC-3 / E-AC-3 (JOC) Dolby Atmos decoding — spatial Atmos for movies, videos and games renders and plays through the platform spatializer.
- AC-4 Dolby Atmos music (Tidal Atmos) — now plays. OnePlus stripped the Dolby DSP topology from this platform's firmware, so the stock AC-4 decoder renders pure silence here (confirmed against a byte-identical same-SoC reference). We replaced it with a custom open-source software AC-4 decoder that does the whole decode on the CPU, bypassing the missing DSP entirely — so immersive-stereo Atmos music (e.g. Tidal) finally has real, audible sound on this hardware. A first for this platform.
- ViPER4Android/FX bundled by default — full system-wide audio effects, no manual install.
- Dolby and ViPER are mutually exclusive — enabling one automatically switches the other off, so they never stack or fight.
- LHDC Bluetooth codec — Savitech's hi-res LHDC (V3 & V5) now works on 15R/A6T, streaming true hi-res audio to compatible earbuds/headphones — a first for this Qualcomm platform, which can't hardware-encode LHDC. Selectable like any other Bluetooth codec, and the everyday codecs (SBC/AAC/LDAC) still use the efficient hardware path.
