Infinity-X — Changelog (1.08.2026)

OOS Camera
- Enabled the libapsfixup camera fixes on 15R (previously A6T-only) — libAlgoProcess now loads apsfixup, so the hi-res P010 capture, HEIF encoding, night-mode (ArcSoft) and deferred-JPEG metadata corrections are active. Brings 15R camera in line with A6T.

Battery & Idle
- New "Aggressive deep sleep" toggle in Settings > Battery (on by default) — drops into deep Doze within minutes of screen-off and keeps it there for much less overnight standby drain. Real-time (high-priority) notifications still come through; only low-priority background pings arrive a little later.
- Per-app Bypass charging, moved out of Game Space into Settings > Battery — choose which apps hold the battery: while a selected app is open and you're plugged in, charging pauses and the level stays put to cut heat and wear, then resumes normally when you leave the app.

Fixes
- Keep custom recovery now works — the Updater option that carries your custom recovery (e.g. OrangeFox) across seamless OTA updates was silently doing nothing and letting stock recovery come back; it now correctly preserves your recovery.
- Back gesture on Low sensitivity fixed — the Low setting was so narrow the back swipe barely registered; the Low/Medium stops are widened to a usable range, so Low is comfortable again.
- Always-portrait lock screen fixed — with auto-rotate off, forcing an app to landscape (via the rotate popup) used to leave the lock screen stuck in landscape; it now correctly snaps back to portrait.
- Camera no longer hangs on the splash — the camera stack was waiting on the OnePlus charger service, which wasn't shipped on 15R; adding it lets the camera open normally.
